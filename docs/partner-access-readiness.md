### Controlled pilot access — operational readiness gate

This checklist is not a contract, acceptance mechanism, or claim that controls are deployed. Wider access should wait for the accountable RF owner to record:

- A versioned deployment contract (route, payload, response and authentication), verified independently of reference examples.
- Authorized organization, tenant, named users, permitted workflows and evaluation period in a private access register.
- Counsel-approved hosted-service terms and evidence of authorized acceptance; public repository licenses are not overridden.
- Credential issuance/revocation ownership, tenant-isolation validation and enforced request/usage limits.
- Incident reporting contact, suspension process, data categories, retention/deletion arrangements and confidentiality scope.
- Pilot assurance limits, a review date and explicit release approval by the accountable owner.

Do not place partner identities, secrets, signed agreements or customer data in this public repository. Public research inspection and use under existing licenses remain separate from hosted-service access. A checklist or terms page does not itself implement rate limiting, isolation or authorization.
